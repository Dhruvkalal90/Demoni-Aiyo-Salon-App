# ✂️ Demoni Aiyo Salon — Live Queue & Token Management App

**Your style, our craft.**  
A real-time virtual queue and token management web application built for **Demoni Aiyo Salon**, designed to eliminate physical waiting lines and streamline chair turnover across specialized grooming stations.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [demoni-salon-flow.base44.app](https://demoni-salon-flow.base44.app)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Remote Token Generation & Live Tracking:** Customers can claim queue tokens remotely, with real-time visibility into active queue counts, wait times, and staff availability.
- **Station-Specific & Transparent Pricing:** Independent queues and upfront pricing/durations for Cutting, Texture, Styling, and Treatment categories.
- **Operational Controls:** Self-service mobile cancellations (up to 15 minutes prior), automated break windows, pre-closing cutoffs, and no-show skip timers.

---

## 💈 Service Categories & Pricing

The salon operates across **4 chairs** offering various services spanning Cutting (₹250–₹550), Texture (₹650–₹1,800), Styling (₹400–₹900), and Treatment (₹700–₹2,200). 
*(You can view the full detailed breakdown of service durations and pricing in the live application or repository data).*

## 📋 Salon Operations & House Rules

- **Location:** Dressrosa Lane, Grand Line (Reception desk inside the main hall)
- **Operating Hours:** Daily, `11:00 – 21:00` *(Afternoon Break: `15:00 – 16:00`)*
- **Token Cutoff:** Tokens stop being issued **45 minutes before closing** and pause during afternoon breaks.
- **Grace Period & Policies:** 10-minute window in the chair before a token skips; mobile cancellations allowed up to 15 minutes prior; all payments settled directly at the reception counter.
